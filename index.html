Our Mission Statement

Education. Opportunity. Equity.

Project EquiFlow works to patch educational and socioeconomic inequities — targeting underrepresented rural and ethnic communities, gender-marginalized groups, and low-income students.

Locally and internationally, we connect students with the resources, mentorship, and tools they need to stay in school and discover what they love.

130M+ Girls Out of School Worldwide

Every year, over 130 million girls are denied their right to an education. We're working to change that — one community at a time.

Our Three Pillars
